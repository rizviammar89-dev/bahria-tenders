# Milestone 0 — Message Templates

Copy-paste and adjust. WhatsApp templates have a **Roman-Urdu** version too — use whichever fits the person. Keep the brand name simple (e.g. a working name like "Bahria Fixit" — change as you like).

---

## 1. Recruit residents (WhatsApp group post)

**English:**
> 🛠️ Need a reliable plumber, electrician, A/C technician or carpenter in Bahria Town?
> Message me — I'll find you a *trusted, vetted* one and get you 2–3 price quotes to choose from. No charge, no hassle. Just tell me what's wrong and your precinct. 🙂

**Roman Urdu:**
> 🛠️ Bahria Town mein acha plumber, electrician, AC technician ya carpenter chahiye?
> Mujhe message karein — main aap ko ek *bharosemand, verified* banda dhoond ke 2–3 rate la ke dunga. Bilkul free. Bas batayein masla kya hai aur aap ka precinct. 🙂

---

## 2. Intake — what to ask the resident

Ask these 4 things (WhatsApp or a Google Form):
1. **What needs fixing?** (short description)
2. **Which trade?** (plumber / electrician / A/C / carpenter / mason / painter)
3. **Your precinct / block?**
4. **When suits you?** (today urgent / this week / flexible)

> *Optional Google Form:* create a free form at forms.google.com with these 4 questions; share the link in the group. (Or just collect over WhatsApp — simpler to start.)

---

## 3. Relay the job to providers

**English:**
> Assalam o Alaikum [Provider]. Job available in [Precinct], Bahria Town:
> "[short description]". When can you go, and what's your price? First to confirm a fair rate gets it. 👍

**Roman Urdu:**
> Assalam o Alaikum [Provider]. [Precinct], Bahria Town mein kaam hai:
> "[masla]". Kab ja sakte ho aur rate kya hoga? Jo pehle theek rate confirm karega usko de dunga. 👍

---

## 4. Relay quotes back to the resident

**English:**
> Got you a few options for your [trade] job:
> 1️⃣ *[Name]* — Rs [price], can come [time]. (Did 3 jobs for neighbours, very tidy.)
> 2️⃣ *[Name]* — Rs [price], [time]. (New but verified ID, keen.)
> 3️⃣ *[Name]* — Rs [price], [time].
> Which one shall I lock in for you?

**Roman Urdu:**
> Aap ke [trade] kaam ke liye kuch options:
> 1️⃣ *[Name]* — Rs [price], [time] aa sakta hai. (Neighbours ke 3 kaam kiye, bohat saaf kaam.)
> 2️⃣ *[Name]* — Rs [price], [time]. (Naya hai par ID verified, kaam ka shauqeen.)
> 3️⃣ *[Name]* — Rs [price], [time].
> Kaunsa lock kar dun aap ke liye?

---

## 5. Post-job follow-up (the most important message)

Send the day after the job. **This is where you capture the real signal.**

**English:**
> Hope the [trade] job went well! 🙏 Quick feedback so I keep good people on my list:
> • Overall, out of 5? ⭐
> • Was he **respectful**? **Tidy** (cleaned up)? **On time**? (each out of 5)
> • Any problem at all? (I'll sort it out if so.)
> And — would you want me to find you people for future jobs too?

**Roman Urdu:**
> Umeed hai [trade] ka kaam acha hua! 🙏 Thori si feedback, taa ke main sirf ache log list pe rakhun:
> • Overall 5 mein se kitne? ⭐
> • Banda **respectful** tha? **Saaf-suthra** (cleanup kiya)? **Time pe** aaya? (har ek 5 mein se)
> • Koi masla to nahi hua? (Hua to main theek karwa dunga.)
> Aur — aage bhi kaam ke liye main aap ko log dhoond ke dun?

---

## 6. If the work was bad (manual dispute)

**To the provider (English):**
> [Provider], the customer in [Precinct] wasn't happy with the [job] — said "[issue]". Can you go back and make it right? It matters for staying on my list. 🙏

**Roman Urdu:**
> [Provider], [Precinct] wale customer kaam se khush nahi the — kehte hain "[masla]". Wapas ja ke theek kar sakte ho? List pe rehne ke liye zaroori hai. 🙏

Then log whether they fixed it (remedied) or not (unresolved) in the tracking sheet — **and note whether the resident valued that you stepped in.** That's your recourse signal.
