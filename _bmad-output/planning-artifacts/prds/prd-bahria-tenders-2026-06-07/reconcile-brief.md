## Genuine gaps / lost intent

### Gap 1 (MODERATE) — Building/society management as partner is materially under-weighted
In the brief this is a **first-class strategic pillar**: one of four "What Makes This Different" bullets, a named stakeholder persona, framed as "partner, not rival," "a distribution and trust channel," "an acquisition engine," and lending "institutional credibility to a young platform." In the PRD it survives only as (a) a §2.2 non-user line and (b) one secondary-channel sentence in the PRD addendum. There is no GTM commitment in the PRD body, no success-metric tie, and it is absent from the moat framing in §1. This is a v1-relevant strategic intent (it is about *acquisition and trust-building at launch*, not a parked Phase-2 feature) that the FR structure largely dropped. Recommend surfacing it in §10 (Operational/GTM) or Success Metrics rather than burying it as a non-user.

### Gap 2 (MINOR–MODERATE) — Resident as a two-person decision unit (housewife + husband) is flattened
The brief (and its addendum personas) is explicit and deliberate: the **housewife** deals with the problem and supervises, the **husband** controls payment and signs off bigger spends — "decisions are made as a couple." The PRD Glossary keeps "housewife and/or husband," but the JTBD (§2.1) and all journeys collapse to a single actor "Ayesha." The *payment-authority / sign-off* dynamic is dropped entirely. Impact is partly muted in v1 because payment is cash/offline (no in-app payer role), so this is not load-bearing for FRs — but it is a real piece of qualitative user understanding that the FR structure lost, and it matters for messaging/UX framing. Worth one line in §2 preserving the couple dynamic.

### Gap 3 (MINOR) — "Turning a community that tolerates bad service into one that demands better" tone is muted
The brief's Solution and vision carry a distinct aspirational/community-transformation register ("a community that quietly tolerates bad service into one that demands and gets better," "Bahria Town talking about it unprompted"). The PRD §1 is faithful but more clinical/mechanical (trust loop, compounding reputation). The *organic advocacy* metric (SM-2) does preserve the "talked about unprompted" signal, so the intent is not lost — only its emotional register is flatter. Low-stakes; flagged only because tone is exactly the kind of thing an FR structure silently loses. No action strictly required.

## Non-gaps explicitly confirmed (so they aren't re-raised)

- Two-mode, 3D Studio, tiered bids, Rising Talent, trust ladder, face-match/crew, surge, maintenance, named vouching, money-back guarantee, multi-community — all **correctly parked** per brief's explicit out-of-v1 list and addendum roadmap. Not gaps.
- "Adab as ranked axis / Gentleman Pro rewards" is Phase-2; v1 Adab-as-score (FR-14) is the right v1 slice.
- 2-month free trial is an *addition* the brief didn't specify numerically, but it is consistent with the brief's "flat provider subscription" + "seed supply first" intent and is justified in the PRD addendum. Not a contradiction.

## Net assessment

No FR-level contradictions or dropped *core mechanics*. The product's mechanical intent is faithfully captured and in places strengthened. The losses are at the **strategic/qualitative layer**: the building-management partnership (most material) and the couple decision-unit nuance, plus a flatter emotional register. None block downstream work; Gap 1 is the one worth a deliberate fix.
