# Brainstorming Session Results

**Facilitator:** Ammar
**Date:** 2026-06-07

## Session Overview

**Topic:** Local home-services marketplace for Bahria Town Karachi — connecting residents with vetted home-service professionals (A/C technicians, plumbers, carpenters, interior designers). Customers browse/compare and shortlist the best providers, then a bidding process determines the engagement.

**Goals:** Generate a wide field of ideas around the bidding model, how customers evaluate "best options," trust and quality assurance, provider onboarding, and what makes the platform sticky within a tight-knit gated community.

### Session Setup

Approach selected: **AI-Recommended Techniques**.

## Technique Selection

**Approach:** AI-Recommended Techniques
**Analysis Context:** Two-sided, trust-heavy, hyper-local home-services marketplace with a bidding mechanic, competing against known patterns (OLX, Foodpanda, Airbnb, local word-of-mouth).

**Recommended Techniques:**

- **Role Playing (Phase 1 — Foundation):** Embody both sides (customer, tradesperson, skeptical villa owner, interior designer) to surface real motivations, fears, and dealbreakers.
- **What If Scenarios (Phase 2 — Idea Generation):** Break the rules of how bidding, trust, and matching could work.
- **Cross-Pollination (Phase 3 — Refinement):** Borrow and adapt proven patterns from other industries/marketplaces into buildable concepts.

**AI Rationale:** Progressive flow from deep empathy → bold divergence → grounded, buildable concepts. Fits the two-sided marketplace, gated-community context, and bidding-model focus. Est. ~70 min.

## Technique Execution Results

### Technique 1: Role Playing

Characters embodied: Ayesha (panicked emergency customer), Bilal (hungry newcomer carpenter), Mrs. Khan (trusted neighbor / human recommendation engine).

**Ideas generated:**

**[Foundation #1]: The Emergency vs. Auction Tension**
- *Concept:* Urgent jobs demand speed, but bidding is inherently slow — the two forces pull apart.
- *Novelty:* Bidding can be a liability in emergencies; implies the platform needs two modes.

**[Foundation #2]: Distance as Trust, Not Just Speed**
- *Concept:* Show each technician's live distance/ETA on a map; proximity predicts speed AND is an unfakeable credibility signal.
- *Novelty:* Reframes geolocation from logistics into a trust feature.

**[Foundation #3]: Two Modes — "Rescue Me" vs. "Court Me"**
- *Concept:* Rescue mode (emergency) ranks by closest/fastest with minimal bidding; Court mode (planned projects like renovation) ranks by portfolio/price/reputation with real competitive bidding.
- *Novelty:* The app morphs based on customer urgency instead of forcing one generic flow.

**[Supply #4]: The Cold-Start Wall**
- *Concept:* Talented newcomers with no local reviews are invisible next to established providers and churn before their first win.
- *Novelty:* Frames the liquidity trap from the provider's emotional POV.

**[Supply #5]: The Race to the Bottom**
- *Concept:* To get noticed, newcomers slash prices, training customers to expect rock-bottom rates and pushing quality providers out.
- *Novelty:* Raw price-bidding actively degrades supply quality.

**[Supply #6]: The "Rising Talent" Lane**
- *Concept:* New providers skip price-bidding for their first 3–5 jobs at a fair fixed rate, badged "New — verified skills," backed by a money-back guarantee, then graduate into open bidding.
- *Novelty:* Solves cold-start and race-to-the-bottom together while protecting price floors.

**[Community #7]: "Your Neighbors Hired Him"**
- *Concept:* Precinct-level social proof — "3 homes in Precinct 10 used Bilal this month."
- *Novelty:* Turns Bahria Town's gated nature into a trust moat competitors can't replicate.

**[Community #8]: Neighbor Vouching (Trusted Locals as Micro-Influencers)**
- *Concept:* Opt-in, name-attached neighbor endorsements; optionally reward active vouchers with referral credit/status.
- *Novelty:* Builds an organic referral flywheel native to a gated community.

### Technique 2: What If Scenarios

**[Mechanic #9]: Blind-to-Price Shortlisting**
- *Concept:* Customers shortlist on distance/rating/vouches/response time before any price shows; bids reveal only after shortlisting.
- *Novelty:* Breaks the OLX "sort-by-cheapest" reflex; frees providers to compete on quality/speed.

**[Mechanic #10]: Bidding With Time, Not Money (Rescue Mode)**
- *Concept:* In emergencies providers compete on commitment — "there in 25 min or call-out free," "I'll hold 6–8pm for you."
- *Novelty:* Matches rescue-mode's true priority (speed); sidesteps slow price-haggling.

**[Safety #11]: The Identity Swap (Bait-and-Switch Worker)** *(problem)*
- *Concept:* Provider builds trust under one identity then sends a different, unvetted person to the job.
- *Novelty:* Attacks the weakest link — who-you-booked vs who-shows-up — high stakes for a woman home alone.

**[Safety #12]: Declared Crew / Verified "+1"**
- *Concept:* Providers register ID-verified helpers; declare who's coming per job; arrival ping shows all faces; undeclared = red flag.
- *Novelty:* Closes the helper loophole without banning apprentices; provider stays accountable for everyone they bring.

**[Quality #13]: The "Adab" Score (Manners as a Ranking Axis)**
- *Concept:* Rate Respect/politeness, Cleanup, and Punctuality separately from technical outcome; rolls into a visible Respect Score.
- *Novelty:* In a modesty-conscious community, manners become a first-class signal — a tidy, respectful provider can out-rank a cheaper rival.

**[Quality #14]: Manners Pay (Soft Scores Unlock Better Jobs)**
- *Concept:* High Respect Scores earn priority placement, a "Gentleman Pro" badge, and premium Court-mode access.
- *Novelty:* Turns courtesy into an economic incentive; trains good home etiquette into the supply base.

**[Onboarding #15]: The Trust Ladder (Gamified Progressive Verification)**
- *Concept:* Level up instead of filling forms. L1 (CNIC+selfie+phone, ~5 min) → receive Rising-Talent jobs immediately; higher levels unlock with portfolio, jobs, Adab scores.
- *Novelty:* Provider starts earning at L1 and earns their way up; onboarding becomes an on-ramp, not a wall.

**[Onboarding #16]: Light Gate, Strong Net**
- *Concept:* Low entry bar (CNIC+selfie); concentrate real safety enforcement at job-time (face-match, declared crew, money-back).
- *Novelty:* Decouples "easy to join" from "safe to hire" — fast supply growth AND safety.

**[Stickiness #17]: Design-It-Then-Bid-It (3D Home Studio)**
- *Concept:* Ayesha designs a room in a HomeByMe-style tool; "Get this built" turns the design into the job spec that designers/carpenters bid on.
- *Novelty:* Collapses imagination → spec → bidding → execution into one pipeline; a reason to open the app for fun. (Build path: crawl=2D planner, walk=embed existing 3D SDK, run=AR, cheat=designer-uploads-mockups.)

**[Stickiness #18]: Self-Serve Design (Ayesha as the Designer)**
- *Concept:* DIY design tool as the hook; her finished design auto-generates the biddable job spec.
- *Novelty:* Zero marginal cost per design + built-in virality; monetize the build, give the design free.

**[Stickiness #19]: Budget-Slider Design**
- *Concept:* Ayesha sets budget first; the studio only surfaces furniture/finishes/layouts that fit, with a live running total.
- *Novelty:* Budget becomes a creative filter and pre-aligns her dream with what carpenters can deliver.

**[Business #20]: Flat Provider Subscription (No Per-Job Commission)**
- *Concept:* Providers pay a flat monthly fee for Pro access and keep 100% of each job — no per-job clip.
- *Novelty:* Removes the per-job sting that drives off-platform leakage; once subscribed, providers want MORE jobs through the app.

**[Business #21]: Monetize the High-Value Design/Renovation Flow**
- *Concept:* Margin comes from Court-mode — paid 3D mockups, material/BOM lists, featured designer placement, premium bid access; rescue-mode stays cheap/free.
- *Novelty:* Charges where intent and ticket size are highest while keeping everyday emergency use friction-free.

### Technique 3: Cross-Pollination

Patterns harvested from proven apps and adapted. (Parked for later: Airbnb Superhost badge, Foodpanda-style customer "BahriaCare" membership, Bark/Thumbtack lead-credits.)

**[Adapt #22]: Uber-Style Live-Arrival Screen (Rescue Mode)**
- *Concept:* Borrow Uber's "driver 4 min away + face + details" screen for emergencies; fuses Distance-as-Trust (#2) and arrival face-match (#11) into one anxiety-killing live view.
- *Novelty:* Proven, instantly familiar UX matched to the panicked rescue-mode customer.

**[Adapt #23]: Tiered "Package" Bids — Basic / Standard / Premium**
- *Concept:* Court-mode bids are structured tiers (Basic = fix only / Standard = fix + warranty / Premium = fix + parts + priority), not one messy number. Becomes the core Court-mode bidding format.
- *Novelty:* Makes bids apples-to-apples comparable, lets quality/manners justify higher tiers, and upsells naturally (borrowed from Fiverr).

**[Adapt #24]: Streak/Completion Mechanics for the Trust Ladder**
- *Concept:* Duolingo/LinkedIn-style "profile 70% complete — 2 steps to Pro" nudges drive providers up the verification ladder (#15).
- *Novelty:* Proven engagement loop applied to provider quality — gamifies getting more trustworthy.

## Idea Organization and Prioritization

### Thematic Organization

**Theme 1 — The Two-Mode Engine (architectural backbone):** #1, #3, #10, #23 — the app behaves differently based on urgency (Rescue vs. Court).

**Theme 2 — Trust & Safety (enforced at job-time):** #2, #11, #12, #22 — safety enforced at the door, not just at signup.

**Theme 3 — Community Trust Moat (unfair advantage):** #7, #8 — Bahria Town's gated, talkative nature = a trust engine clones can't copy.

**Theme 4 — Provider Success & Anti-Race-to-Bottom:** #4, #5, #6, #9, #13, #14 — providers win on speed, manners, reputation, not just lowest price.

**Theme 5 — Frictionless Onboarding:** #15, #16, #24 — start earning at Level 1, earn your way up.

**Theme 6 — The Design Studio (breakout differentiator):** #17, #18, #19 — imagination → spec → bid → build pipeline; sticky, viral hook.

**Theme 7 — Business Model:** #20, #21 — disintermediation-resistant; providers keep 100%, platform earns on subscription + high-value design.

### Prioritization Results

**MVP (build first — core loop):**
1. Two-mode core (#3) with Rescue mode = geolocation + live-arrival face-match (#2, #10, #22)
2. Light onboarding (#15/#16) — CNIC + selfie
3. Neighbor social proof (#7)
4. Flat provider subscription (#20)

**Phase 2 (differentiators):**
5. Design Studio — 2D + budget slider (#17/#18/#19) → tiered Court-mode bids (#23)
6. Adab Score + Manners Pay (#13/#14); Rising Talent lane (#6)
7. Declared crew (#12); neighbor vouching (#8); monetize design flow (#21)

**Parked:** Airbnb Superhost badge, customer membership, lead-credits.

**Three real differentiators (clone-proof):** Design-to-Bid Studio (#17), the Adab/manners economy (#13/#14), and hyper-local neighbor trust (#7/#8).
